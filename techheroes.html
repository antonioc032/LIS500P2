Here is our tech hero!

Allison Scott

Dr. Allison Scott is definitely someone worth learning about. She is the CEO of the Kapor Foundation which focuses on giving access and opportunities to those who need them. They stride to make technology fields more equitable and fight against racial and economic inequality. Dr. Scott specializes in the research aspect of advancing equity. She has done a lot of work on making computer science education more accessible, especially for women of color. You can learn more about her and her company by following these links:
