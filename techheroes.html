Here are our tech heroes!

Amelia's Tech Hero:

Right now, my tech hero is Melanie Perkins, the CEO of Canva. Canva is a platform that makes graphic design accessible to everyone. I am inspired by Melanie because she combined creativity, technology, and communication, and used it to change the way people create and share digital content. Her success also shows how a creative idea can grow into a technology that millions of people use every day.

Antonio's Tech Hero:

Although I hadn't heard of her or her company before starting researching, Dr. Allison Scott is definitely someone worth learning about. She is the CEO of the Kapor Foundation which focuses on giving access and opportunities to those who need them. She has done a lot of work on making computer science education more accessible, especially for women of color. You can find a link to a page about her and her company here: Allison Scott Kapor Foundation
