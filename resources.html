Definitions:

Internet-Based Technology (IBT): technology that uses many interconnected networks to provide a digital platform for communication through transferring data.

Intersectionality: The idea that different parts of a person's upbringing and identity, such as race, gender, and class, can impact how they experience the world differently.

Implicit Bias: The preconcieved beliefs that people have that may not be recognized. The beliefs are usually assumptions about different groups pf people.
