Resource Guide

Gain a better understanding of the language used to talk about code and power

Definitions:

Internet-Based Technology (IBT): technology that uses many interconnected networks to provide a digital platform for communication through transferring data. Not everyone has equal access to IBT and there's still a lot of work to make important technology accessible to everyone around the world who needs them. Learn more by visiting this link: Digital Divides

Intersectionality: The idea that different parts of a person's upbringing and identity, such as race, gender, and class, can impact how they experience the world differently. You can learn a lot more about intersectionality in depth by visiting this link: Intersectionality 101

Implicit Bias: The preconcieved beliefs that people have that may not be recognized. The beliefs are usually assumptions about different groups pf people. You can learn more about your own implicit biases by visiting this link, but don't think of it as a clear description of your non-concious thoughts, it's just to get you thinking: Harvard IAT
